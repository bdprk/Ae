Measured effect
  No generated C, executable or cache change: emit-c output is byte-identical
  between the constrained and unconstrained probe sources (20,352 bytes),
  -O0 executable 64,195 bytes.
  Audit timing (median of 7 alternating `check` runs, wall clock ms):
    200 declarations: with 113.4 | without 103.1 | delta 10.3
    800 declarations: with 1433.0 | without 1338.8 | delta 94.2
  The delta is an UPPER BOUND, not the pure audit cost: the constrained source
  has two extra tokens per line, and GenericFn still clones the whole token
  stream per declaration (O(N^2), pre-existing since ADR-31). Load varies a
  lot on this machine; do not read these as a speedup, nor compare absolute
  numbers with the w3 / winhdr archives.
  Four scenarios (ms): cold 412.6 | unchanged 118.2 | impl 370.1 | iface 391.7

Boundaries (do not overstate)
  - This is NOT trait/impl. No method table, no static dispatch, no orphan
    rule, no user-defined constraints. Every instantiation is still fully
    type-checked (ADR-33).
  - The check can only reject. Unprovable cases are skipped and reported by
    the per-instantiation check; unconstrained generics keep old behaviour.
  - It is a deliberate tightening: a weak constraint now fails at the
    declaration even if only valid types would ever be used. Migration is to
    narrow the constraint or drop it.
  - Not claimed: zero compile-time regression, an exact ratio, or any change
    to runtime memory/CPU.
